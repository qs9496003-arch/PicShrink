# PicShrink Privacy

PicShrink is a 100% client-side image tool. Your privacy is simple:

- **No file ever leaves your device.** Every tool — resize, crop, filters, background eraser, drawing, watermark, collage, QR, palette, EXIF — runs entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API.
- **No account.** PicShrink does not ask you to sign up or sign in.
- **No uploads.** Images are never sent to any server, ours or anyone else's.
- **No tracking in the app.** The app itself sets no analytics cookies and stores nothing except your theme choice (light/dark) in your browser's local storage.
- **Sample images.** The built-in sample photos load from Unsplash when you choose one; that is the only network request the app makes besides loading the page itself.
